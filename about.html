About Reem

Meet your guide to learning Arabic and the Quran

Who I Am

I’m Reem, an experienced educator from Egypt, dedicated to teaching the Arabic language and the Quran to both Arabic and non-Arabic speakers. With a deep passion for sharing the beauty of Arabic culture and Islamic knowledge, I offer engaging online courses and resources tailored to learners worldwide.

Education

  • Bachelor’s Degree in Art and Education, South Valley University, 2005–2009

Certifications

  • Ijaza in Quran Recitation (Hafs from Asem Narration), 1st Place in Mallawi Center, 2nd Place in Minya Governorate, 2018
  • Ijaza in Quran Recitation for Qira’at: Asem, Ibn Amer, Al-Kesa’ey, Ibn Katheer, Qaloon Narration
  • Ijaza in At-Tuhfa and Al-Jazariyya (Tajweed Texts)
  • Certificate in Arabic Grammar (Explanation of Al-Ajrumiyyah)
  • Certificate in Quran Science and Work Project
  • Certificate in Teaching Arabic to Non-Arabic Speakers

Experience

  • Private Quran, Arabic, and Islamic Studies Teacher, 2017–Present
  • Freelance Transcriptionist (Arabic and English Videos/Audio), 2021–Present
